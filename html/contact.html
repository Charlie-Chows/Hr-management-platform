Before you write

A few quick answers.

If your question isn't here, just ask — we'd rather hear it than guess.

Do you work with teams outside India?

Yes. We currently work with partner teams across twelve countries, with support tailored to local practices and compliance needs.

Is there a minimum team size?

Not really. Our Essential plan suits small, close-knit teams, and everything scales from there as you grow.

Can I see a demo before deciding?

Of course. Mention "demo" in your message and we'll set up a short, unhurried walkthrough at a time that suits you.

How do you handle my information?

Your enquiry is treated with the same care we give our partners' people data. Nothing is shared, and nothing is added to a list without your consent.
