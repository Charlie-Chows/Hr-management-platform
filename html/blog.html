The cost of a decision made twice

Most teams don't lose time to hard problems. They lose it to problems that were already solved once — and quietly solved again, in a different room, by different people, with slightly different answers.

This piece looks at why repeated decisions are so expensive, how to write them down so they stay decided, and why the smallest habit of recording context is worth more than any tool.
